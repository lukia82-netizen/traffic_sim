## 2026-07-21 - Grouping Vehicle Snapshots by Lane and Path to Eliminate O(N^2) Complexity
**Learning:** During tick updates, oncoming checks, leader lookups, and conflict point evaluations were previously performing full linear scans O(N) over all vehicle snapshots. By pre-grouping snapshots into lane-indexed and path-indexed array structures at the start of each frame, we reduced complexity to O(M) where M is the number of active vehicles in that specific lane or path, yielding an immediate ~25% simulation speedup.
**Action:** When working on spatial/agent-based simulations, avoid linear scans across the entire agent population for localized checks (e.g. oncoming lanes or path-specific conflicts). Pre-grouping or indexing agents at the frame's boundary avoids redundant filtering operations.
