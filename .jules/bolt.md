## 2026-02-18 - Early-Exit and Heap-Allocation Bottlenecks in Spatial Partitioning
**Learning:** Under typical or sparse simulation conditions, early-exit checks (such as distance horizon vs braking distance) prevent the majority of vehicles from ever reaching the core nested conflict loops of `evaluate_conflict_point`. Consequently, eagerly allocating helper structures like `Vec`s for spatial/path grouping introduces heap allocator overhead that outweighs the actual loop cost, leading to 0.8x regression. Only by utilizing zero-allocation/reusable arrays and ensuring active simulation states are reached did we unlock the true 1.6x speedup.
**Action:** Always verify if code paths are early-exiting in benchmarks or production, and prefer zero-allocation stack storage or object pools when writing spatial/lane grouping partitions.
